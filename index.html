About Ministen

Structured questionnaires, kept in one place

Ministen is a simple application that allows users to complete structured questionnaires related to myasthenia gravis and quality of life. The information entered can be reviewed over time within the app.

Users may choose to export their information from the app. Any decision to share an exported file remains under the user's control.

Privacy by design

Your information stays with you.

01

Stored locally

Information is kept locally on the user's device.

02

No central database

Ministen does not operate a central database of users' health information.

03

Export under your control

Users decide if and when to export their information.
