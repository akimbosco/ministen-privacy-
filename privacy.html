Privacy Policy

Ministen is designed to keep the user's information on their own device. This policy explains the app's approach to data and the privacy of this website.

1. Information in the app

Ministen allows users to enter information in structured questionnaires related to myasthenia gravis and quality of life. Depending on the features used, this may include questionnaire responses and information entered by the user about their daily experience.

Ministen does not collect this information in a central health-data database and does not automatically send it to Ministen.

2. Local storage

Information entered or generated in the app is stored locally on the user's device. Ministen does not provide a cloud account or central synchronization service for this information.

Users are responsible for controlling access to their device and for any device-level backup settings they choose to enable.

3. Device permissions

Some optional app features may request access to device capabilities or health and activity information made available by the operating system. Access occurs only after the user grants the relevant permission and can be changed in the device settings.

Information accessed through these optional features is used on the device and is not sent to a Ministen server.

4. Export and sharing

The app may allow users to create an export of their information. Exporting is initiated by the user. Ministen does not automatically transmit exported files or decide who receives them.

Once a user chooses to share or store an exported file outside the app, the handling of that copy depends on the service, person or location selected by the user.

5. Deleting information

Users can delete information stored locally through the controls available in the app. Removing the app may also remove its locally stored information, subject to the operating system and any device backup settings.

6. This website

This static website does not use analytics, advertising technology or tracking cookies. It does not include contact forms or collect health information.

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. As the hosting provider, GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service under its own terms and privacy documentation.
